About Us

Where Southern Hospitality Meets Executive Excellence

Holy City Executive Solutions was born from a simple belief — that behind every successful leader is a seamless support system. Based in Charleston, South Carolina, we bring the warmth and attentiveness of the Holy City to executive-level virtual assistance.

We partner with entrepreneurs, C-suite executives, and growing businesses who need more than a task manager — they need a trusted partner who anticipates needs, eliminates friction, and keeps everything running beautifully.

Hi, I'm Celeste — founder of Holy City Executive Solutions. I served in the United States Navy and earned my MBA from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill's Kenan-Flagler Business School.
